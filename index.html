Bunny Bounce

Now in testing

A top-down garden game about a very hungry bunny. Tap veggies to eat them, collect 57 bunnies, breed babies in the nursery and fill your own Bunny Farm.

No ads. No accounts. No data collected.

Coming to iPhone, iPad and Android. Want to play it early? Join the test and tell us what you think.

Android testing in three taps

  1. 1Join the group

Open the page below on your Android phone or tablet and tap its three big buttons in order, with the same Google account for all three. Google Play needs 12 testers for 14 days, so please keep it installed for two weeks.

Test on Android Test on iPhone or iPad

On iPhone or iPad, install Apple's TestFlight app first, then open the link and tap Accept. Adults only; kids are welcome to play on your device. We'll never ask for a password or a code.
